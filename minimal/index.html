Where every visit begins

Every arrival starts at the gate. So should good security.

Residents drive in, guests are expected, deliveries pile up and the milkman comes before sunrise. NXT Gate keeps track of all of it. The barrier knows which cars belong, the guard knows who is expected, and the app keeps a record of everyone who came and went.

Most gate apps stop at the guard's phone. Most barrier companies stop at the motor. We make both, so they work together from day one.
