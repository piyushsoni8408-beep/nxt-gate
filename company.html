About NXT Gate

Why a vehicle maker builds gates

Every gate is a machine. A boom barrier is a motor, a controller and a mechanism that has to work thousands of times a day in sun, dust and monsoon. Tunwal has spent years building exactly that kind of hardware for Indian roads.

NXT Gate brings that engineering to access control. We supply boom barriers, ANPR cameras and safety sensors, and connect them to the Gate Tech app, so residents, guards and administrators work from one system.

Because hardware and software come from one company, there is one team responsible for the gate, from the first site survey to every service visit after.

Tunwal E-Motors

Two decades of building trust

Incorporated in 2018, Tunwal E-Motors was founded to help protect the environment and reduce pollution across India. Its roots go back over two decades to Tunwal Electronics in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.

Today Tunwal designs and manufactures 10+ electric two- and three-wheelers at its plant in Palsana, Rajasthan, with a sales and service network of 400+ dealers across 19 states.
